The channel

Cold Case FC is a YouTube channel that tells true, fact-checked football stories in under a minute: the strangest matches, the most impossible records and the mysteries football never solved. A new Short every day, with subtitles in 14 languages. Sources for every story are listed in each video's description.

Cold Case FC Uploader

This site also documents Cold Case FC Uploader, the private tool the channel uses to publish its own videos. It signs in to the channel's own YouTube account with Google OAuth and uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, captions, translations, thumbnails and playlists, and to update the channel page.

The tool is used only by the channel's owner and is not offered to anyone else. How it handles data is explained in the Privacy Policy.
